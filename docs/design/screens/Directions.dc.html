Three looks for DRAKON

Direction A is on every page and in the design system. B and C are here to compare, one small sample each. To switch, tell Claude “switch DRAKON to direction B” (or C). Prices, Cash on Delivery and every business rule stay the same in all three.

Applied to every page

A. Ember Gallery, with gallery labels

Mood
A private night gallery for collectors. Each piece sits alone under one warm light, with a small label that says what it is, if it is a limited run, and what is left.
Colors
Midnight ground, bone white text, steel greys. Ember orange only on the logo and the one main button of a screen.
Type
Outfit only, with a proper Taka sign from Noto Sans Bengali. Big tight headlines, even-width numbers on every price, small steel labels.
Photos
Top-down on dark slate, one warm light from the upper left, long soft shadows, matte materials. Collection tiles are close-ups, never repeats.
Motion
One hero reveal on load. Images fade in once. A slow zoom on photo hover. Nothing loops.

B. Floodlight Matchday

Mood
Football under stadium lights. Louder, faster, more energy for match-night fans and gamers.
Colors
Same midnight, but orange in big blocks: price tags, number plates, section bands.
Type
Adds Saira Condensed for caps headlines and jersey-size numbers; Outfit for body.
Photos
Same shots, cropped tighter and harder, with pitch-line graphics over them.
Motion
Snappy slides and counters. More movement, less calm.

C. Night Terminal

Mood
A coder's desk at 3 a.m. Spec sheets, serial numbers, visible grids. Precise and nerdy.
Colors
Near-black midnight with grid lines; orange only as a cursor and live status.
Type
Adds JetBrains Mono for labels, prices and codes; Outfit for headlines.
Photos
Same shots in square frames with corner crop marks, like a product spec sheet.
Motion
A blinking cursor, text that types in once. Square corners everywhere.
